Overview

Q8 Logistics is the fastest-growing logistics platform in the GCC. Founded in 2014 by Yousef Al-Mutawa, we operate across Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates — moving freight, packages, and partnerships at scale. Today: 400+ people, 200+ vehicles in our fleet, 3 country operations, and partnerships with most major retail and e-commerce brands across MENA. We started in a meeting room in Hawalli with one truck. Today we move 50,000+ packages a day across the region. Hiring engineers, drivers, and operations leads. We believe logistics is a craft.
