About

Before Q8, I spent 6 years at Zain Kuwait building digital campaigns that won me an MBA-level masterclass in "ship fast, learn faster." Outside work I'm a mom to a small human named Layla who turned 7 this year and asks me approximately 400 questions a day, wife to my favorite person Khaled, and full-time staff to one rescue cat named Loki (yes, like the Marvel one — Layla picked the name in 2019, I had no veto power).
