01 — About

A craft, not just a service.

Q8 Logistics started in October 2014 in a meeting room in Hawalli with one truck and one part-time driver. The thesis was simple: the GCC deserved a logistics company built locally, run with craft, and accountable to its customers.

Ten years later we move 50,000+ packages a day across Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates. Four hundred people on the team. Two hundred vehicles. Three countries. One philosophy.

We believe logistics is a craft. We believe MENA founders should build for MENA first. We believe shipping is a more honest measure than vision decks.

Our customers are e-commerce platforms, manufacturers, hospitals, and the hundreds of small businesses that built the Gulf's modern economy. Our work is making sure their shipments arrive — on time, intact, and tracked end-to-end.

"We're a logistics company. The job is to move things from A to B without drama. Every other claim should have to earn that one first."
Yousef Al-Mutawa · Founder & CEO
