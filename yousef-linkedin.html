About

Founder & CEO of Q8 Logistics — building MENA's logistics backbone from Kuwait City. Started Q8 in October 2014 from a meeting room in Hawalli with one truck and one part-time driver. Today we move 50,000+ packages a day across Kuwait, KSA, and UAE — 400+ people, 200+ vehicles, three country operations. Before Q8 I spent 8 years at Agility Logistics learning how freight actually works (harder than it looks). Before that I was a finance grad at Kuwait University who thought I'd end up at NBK. I believe logistics is a craft. I believe MENA founders should build for MENA first. I believe shipping is a more honest measure than vision decks. Outside work I'm husband to Hessa, dad to two kids who are growing too fast (Saud, born 2016, and Dana, born 2019), Real Madrid fan since 1998, and weekend hiker. Born and raised in Kuwait City. 📩 See Contact info  •  DMs open for founders, freight folks, or coffee in Kuwait.
